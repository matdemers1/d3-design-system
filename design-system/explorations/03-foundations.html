Voice the short version

CasingSentence case everywhere. Capitals for the first letter and proper nouns only.
DatesRelative to 7 days, absolute after. The month is always a word. Never bare toLocaleDateString().
NumbersOne shared formatter. Tabular figures. 184 ms, never 0.18 s.
ErrorsWhat happened · why · what to do next, as a control.
Empty statesHeading · context · action. "No items" is a status code, not an empty state.
ButtonsVerb first. Destructive buttons name the object and the count. Cancel is always "Cancel".
